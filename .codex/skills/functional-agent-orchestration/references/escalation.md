# Escalation

Analyst begins at Luna high. Escalate to Sol medium, then Sol high when confidence stays low, evidence conflicts, cross-domain reasoning is required, diagnosis repeats, or impact is high. Preserve the earlier evidence and open questions at each handoff.

Planner may use Sol high for complex or high-risk planning. Executor never upgrades itself; return failures to Analyst and Planner. Escalation changes reasoning capacity, not authorization. If a user decision is essential, present the reviewed artifact and ask for that decision.
